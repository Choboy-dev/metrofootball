# AI Teams Page Overrides

> **PROJECT:** Metro Football
> **Page Type:** Premium season long AI squad tracker (four philosophies)

> Rules here override `MASTER.md` for `/ai-teams`.

---

## Purpose

Four AI squads locked from Gameweek 2 and nurtured until end of season. Same journey for every Premium member. Premium users browse any gameweek from GW2 onward.

## Philosophies

1. **Value** Best affordable picks in each position
2. **Attackers** Premium forwards first
3. **Midfielders** Premium midfielders first
4. **Defenders** Premium defenders first

## FPL rules (strict)

- Squad cost at or under £100.0m
- Max 3 players per club
- 1 free transfer per gameweek, bank up to 5
- Extra transfers cost 4 points each
- Selling price: half profit on rises, full loss on drops
- Chips: Bench Boost, Triple Captain, Free Hit (once each until FPL grants more)
- Chips only on a standout gameweek for that squad, not by default early in the season
- No Wildcard (squads evolve through transfers instead)

## Layout

- Season intro plus gameweek prev or next controls
- Philosophy tabs (pill group)
- Meta row: strength, predicted or points (after GW finishes), team value, free transfers
- Compact chip note pill when a chip was played that week
- Transfer list when transfers happened
- Pick style pitch plus bench with FDR and fixtures
- No red eyebrows, no large chip panels

## Motion

- Soft stagger on pitch cards when switching tabs or gameweeks
- Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Copy

- We / Metro Football voice
- Simple enough for a 10 year old
- No dashes, no exclamation marks

- Premium only. Login required. Shared season file for all Premium members.
