# Chip Strategy Planner Overrides

> **PROJECT:** Metro Football
> **Page Type:** Premium personalised chip planner

> Rules here override `MASTER.md` for `/chip-strategy`.

---

## Purpose

Help Premium users plan when to play each FPL chip based on their linked squad. Updates whenever their team changes on Pick.

## Layout

1. Soft header with team name and planning gameweek
2. Four chip cards (inventory + target week + status)
3. AI summary line for the squad right now
4. Horizontal gameweek timeline with heat bars per chip
5. Detail panel for the selected chip (top weeks + plain reason)

## Visual

- Clean white cards, subtle tints on active chip
- Each chip has its own colour: Bench Boost red, Triple Captain gold, Free Hit blue, Wildcard purple
- Timeline shows one colour per week only when a chip is recommended; blank weeks stay empty
- Timeline uses scroll snap, no cramped labels

## Copy

- We / Metro Football voice
- Simple enough for a 10 year old
- No dashes, no exclamation marks
- Say predicted points, never project or projected
- Short sentences that sound like normal advice

## Access

- Premium only, linked FPL team required
