# Mini Leagues Overrides

> **PROJECT:** Metro Football
> **Page Type:** Premium mini league tracker

> Rules here override `MASTER.md` for `/mini-leagues`.

---

## Purpose

Help Premium users watch FPL classic leagues they already play in. Standings, rank swings, rivals, and recent rival moves.

## Layout

1. Header with gameweek and league name
2. League rail to switch tracked leagues and add a new one
3. Summary card with one plain brief
4. Four stat tiles: rank, total points, gap to leader, rank change
5. Standings table with movement arrows
6. Side stack: rivals to watch, biggest movers, recent rival transfers
7. Alerts toggle per league

## Visual

- Premium clean cards, soft red accent on your row
- Rank up green tint, rank down muted red
- No red eyebrow labels

## Copy

- We / Metro Football voice
- Simple enough for a 10 year old
- No dashes, no exclamation marks
- Say predicted points only where relevant

## Access

- Premium only, linked FPL team required
