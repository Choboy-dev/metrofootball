What is in a profile

  • Form against expectation

    Points next to expected points for every gameweek, so a hot streak is separable from a sustainable one.

  • The underlying numbers

    Shots, big chances and key passes, each ranked against every other player in the same position.

  • Defensive contribution

    How often a player clears the threshold that banks two points, and how close they get when they miss.

  • What is coming up

    The next six fixtures with difficulty, predicted points and expected minutes.
