How to use our gameweek tips

Each gameweek we publish one clear briefing before the deadline. It covers who to captain, who to bench and the moves we think are worth making, written for managers who want a straight answer.

What the briefing covers

The briefing starts with the captain, because no other decision moves your score as much. It looks at the fixtures, form and minutes behind the main options and says which way we lean. Then it covers players to bench or avoid, transfer targets at different prices and anything else that matters that week, from rotation worries to doubles and blanks. Older briefings stay online, so you can look back at what we said and how it turned out.

How to use it

  1. Read it alongside the press conferences. Weigh our view against the freshest team news before you commit.
  2. Use it as a check, not a script. If our captain differs from yours, read the reasons and decide which argument you trust more.
  3. Take the warnings seriously. Avoiding a trap is often worth as much as finding a bargain.
  4. Look back at past weeks. You will see where our thinking worked and where it did not, which helps you judge how much weight to give it.

Why we write it this way

Plenty of FPL content hands you twenty captain options and leaves you more confused than before. We would rather commit to a view and explain it. Sometimes we will be wrong, because football does not follow a script, but a clear argument is far easier to test against your own thinking than a long list of maybes. For the reasoning we use every week, read our guide on who to captain in FPL.

To apply the same thinking to your own players, use the Pick page or AI Tips. Injuries are covered in our weekly injury digest.
